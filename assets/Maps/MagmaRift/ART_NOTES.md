# Magma Rift asset provenance

`basalt-floor.png` and `basalt-cover.png` were created with the built-in image_gen tool without reference images. The map does not use older game images. The lava material and its final prompt are documented in LAVA_ART.md.

## Basalt ground final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production seamless square GROUND MATERIAL TEXTURE for a polished 2D cartoon arena game, not a full map or illustration. Create a fresh original image with no references. Strict orthographic directly overhead. Dark cooled volcanic basalt paving: large organically fitted irregular charcoal-blue slabs, subtle cool-gray angular bevels, a small number of fine narrow dark cracks, broad smooth quiet surfaces for clear character visibility. Flat hand-painted game art with controlled 3-4 tone shading, clean edges and appealing deliberate shapes. Medium detail, NOT photo-realistic, NOT gritty, NOT overpainted, NOT repetitive tiny triangles or uniform floor tiles. No bright lava or glowing seams. Large slabs roughly 120-240 pixels across, each with a different simple 5-8 sided silhouette. Low overall contrast with only restrained thin highlight edges. Material fills the entire square edge to edge and tiles seamlessly in both directions: opposite edges must connect naturally with consistent illumination. No perspective, no horizon, no isolated boulder, no borders, no vignettes, no cast shadows, no text, no grid, no characters, no symbols. This is only flat walkable ground texture.

## Basalt cover final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE original transparent-background basalt boulder sprite for a polished cartoon 2D arena shooter. No input references. Three-quarter top-down game view with a clearly readable low broad silhouette, an angular asymmetric cluster of three interlocked volcanic rocks, dark charcoal blue lower faces and medium slate blue upper facets, restrained warm brown accents near the base. Clean deliberate large polygonal planes with thin crisp dark outlines and 3-4 controlled tones; matches hand-drawn cartoon characters, not a photorealistic painting. A few small surface chips, one narrow orange fissure between the rocks, no surrounding floating debris. A low wide piece of cover, approximately width to height 1.3:1. Entire object fully visible, large and centered with transparent padding, genuine alpha background. Flat clean polished game sprite, no photographic texture or messy brush noise. No ground plane, no rectangular background, no label, no letters, no weapons, no characters, no border, no tall tower. Very subtle contact shadow only immediately beneath the object, preserve transparent outside.
