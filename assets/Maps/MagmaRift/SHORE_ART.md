# Basalt shoreline

Saved asset: `basalt-rim.png`. Created using the built-in image_gen tool without reference images. `shore.tsx` registers the asset, while the `shore` object layer places it along every lava boundary. The renderer crops transparent padding and overlaps varied strip sections over corners, after rendering lava. Stone-bank collision occupies the adjacent 18 world units on the land side of the same boundary. The stones remain visible when effects are disabled.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original transparent game sprite strip for the ROCKY SHORELINE of a lava pool in a 2D top-down arena shooter. Generate a single long horizontal continuous row of closely interlocked chunky basalt stones, approximately 6:1 width-to-height silhouette, centered with transparent padding. The row consists of 10 to 14 distinct irregular angular volcanic rocks touching and slightly overlapping, varied sizes but all low broad shapes, no large gaps. Camera almost overhead with a small amount of visible dark side faces, suitable for a walkable floor edge. Clean polished hand-painted cartoon game style, controlled large slate-blue gray upper facets, charcoal dark lower faces, crisp readable silhouettes, a little warm orange reflected light along the lower rim and a few fine glowing orange gaps BETWEEN stones. Mostly solid stone, no lava surface or ground plane. The connected stone row must be readable as a continuous rugged natural rocky bank, not identical bricks, not a straight metal outline, not a tall wall, not a tower, not a necklace of circles. Art palette matches dark blue charcoal volcanic terrain and stylized 2D characters. Each stone has a deliberately distinct 5-8 sided form with 3-4 shading tones and restrained edge highlights. No gritty photographic detail or painterly noise. Full object visible, actual transparent background outside the strip, subtle contact shadow only directly under stones. No text, letters, borders, UI, characters, weapons, scenery or surrounding debris. Strictly ONE horizontal rock shoreline strip.
