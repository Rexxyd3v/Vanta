# VANTA 1.0.0

## Download and play

Download **VANTA-1.0.0-Setup.exe** below and run it. The installer includes the game, all maps and assets, and the required runtime DLLs. No separate ZIP extraction, MSYS2, compiler, or asset download is needed.

Install for your Windows account, then launch **VANTA** from the Start menu. A desktop shortcut is optional. Administrator access is not required for the default installation.

**System:** 64-bit Windows 10/11, OpenGL 3.3-capable graphics. A microphone is optional for proximity voice chat.

## Included

- Offline play with Training, Free for All, Team Deathmatch, Elimination, and Deathmatch / Flag.
- Up to 15 offline bots with Easy, Medium, and Difficult settings.
- Online host/join rooms and proximity push-to-talk voice chat.
- Magma Rift, Abandoned Foundry, and Desert Outpost arenas.
- Eight guns, default finishes and skins, three saved bags, and melee weapons.
- Dash, shield, frag grenades, spreading poison smoke, and flashbangs.
- Updated lobby and gameplay UI, reload and ability indicators, and weapon-icon kill feed.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the installer checksum. GitHub's automatically generated source-code archives are for developers; players only need the **Setup.exe** asset.
